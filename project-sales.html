01 · Analysis overview

Where are the strongest opportunities to grow sales?

SalesPro Co. needed a clear view of sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and geographic opportunity.

The analysis connects monthly trends, product sub-categories, customer demographics, commute distance, and country-level performance to business-ready recommendations.

Built from SQL analysis and translated into dashboard-ready insight for decision makers.

03 · Product performance

Mountain bikes are the primary revenue engine

The highest-selling categories account for the largest share of revenue, while lower-contributing categories point to cross-sell and review opportunities.

Top sub-category

$6.34M in Mountain Bike sales led all product sub-categories.

Road Bikes ($5.20M) and Touring Bikes ($3.82M) also drive significant revenue. Tires and Tubes and Helmets warrant a targeted product strategy.

04 · Customer insights

Core customers are 30 to 50 years old

Customer behavior highlights where the current business is strongest—and which segments offer room for growth.

Core segment

Customers aged 30–40 generated 23,463 total sales, followed by ages 40–50 with 16,584.

The current core merits continued loyalty investment, while tailored outreach can develop younger and 60+ customer segments.

Households with children made up 69.04% of customers, supporting family-oriented products, marketing, and promotions.

05 · Customer behavior

Shorter commutes correspond to higher sales

Local customers are the strongest opportunity for targeted engagement and proximity-focused messaging.

Local opportunity

Customers travelling 0–1 miles generated 5,929 sales—the largest commute-distance group.

Sales decline with longer commute distances. Local promotions and convenient pickup or service experiences can strengthen loyalty.
